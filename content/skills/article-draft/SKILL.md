Draft, from the content map entry.

- **Answer the question in the first paragraph.** Do not build up to it. Readers who wanted the answer leave; readers who wanted depth keep going.
- **Specifics over generalities.** Real numbers, real code, real screenshots. If you can't be specific, the article isn't ready.
- **Structure for scanning** — descriptive headings, short paragraphs.
- **Internal links** to the two or three most related pages.
- Add schema and a canonical.

Flag anything you wrote that the user can't personally vouch for. Publishing unverified claims under their name is their risk, and they should know where it is.
