Plan one channel properly rather than five badly.

1. **Pick one** based on where the ICP already is, not on what's fashionable. Justify the pick in one sentence.
2. **Design the test.** What's being tried, at what volume, over what period. Small enough to afford, big enough to read.
3. **Set the budget** — and check it against the CAC ceiling from `/unit-economics`. If the channel can't clear the ceiling, say so now rather than after the money's gone.
4. **Name the go/no-go number** before starting. The single result that means keep going, and the one that means stop.
5. **Say what happens on a no.** A channel plan without a stop condition is how budgets disappear.

Write to `02-b2c/README.md`.
