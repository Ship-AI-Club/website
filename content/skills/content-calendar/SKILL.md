description: Build a four-week calendar from what the ICP actually asks, kept as a live artifact you update rather than regenerate. Use once voice and ICP exist.
---

Build four weeks of content the user will actually publish.

**Read first:** the ICP profile and the voice skill in `01-voice/`. If the profile isn't there, stop and run `/icp-profile` — a calendar built without one is a list of topics, and topics are what people abandon in week two.

1. **Start from questions, not keywords.** What does this specific person ask before they buy, during, and after. One question per entry. If you can't picture someone typing it, cut it.
2. **Set a cadence they'd still hit in month four.** Two a week beats five a week for eleven days. Under-promise the schedule on purpose; the impressive one is the one that gets abandoned.
3. **Four weeks, dated.** Each entry names the question, the format, the channel, and the one thing it should make someone do.
4. **Mark what needs proof.** Any entry resting on a number, a customer name or a screenshot gets flagged now, while there's still time to go and get it.
5. **Leave two slots empty a month.** Something worth writing about will happen, and a full calendar is the reason nobody writes it.

**Then make it live.** A calendar generated once and never touched is dead the day it's made. Before you finish: add one entry by hand, move one, delete one. Write the rule into the top of the file — this artifact gets edited every week, never regenerated. Regenerating throws away everything the user learned since the last version, which is the only part that was ever worth having.

Write to `02-routines/calendar.md`.
