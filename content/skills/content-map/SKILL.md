---

Build a content map that's a distribution system, not a blog.

1. **Start from the ICP**, not from keywords. What does this specific person type when they have this specific problem?
2. **Group by intent** — problem-aware, solution-aware, comparison, and post-purchase. Different pages, different jobs.
3. **Prioritise by winnability × value.** A term you can rank for that converts beats a high-volume term you can't.
4. **First twenty pages, ordered.** With the first one drafted before the session ends.
5. Write to `05-site/README.md`.

Refuse to produce a list of 200 keywords. Twenty pages that get written beats two hundred that don't.
