---
name: icp-builder
description: Define a B2B ICP tight enough to write an email to, and build the named account list. Use at session 03, Outbound Agents.
---

Build an ICP you can act on today.

1. **Three dimensions:** company (size, sector, stage), role (the person who feels the pain, and the person who signs — often different), and trigger event (what makes this urgent now).
2. **The test:** name twenty real companies that fit. If you can't, the ICP is still too broad. Do this before writing any copy.
3. **Build the list** — twenty named accounts with the specific person at each, and the trigger you think applies.
4. **Disqualify explicitly.** Who is *not* the ICP, so time doesn't leak into bad-fit conversations.
5. Write to `03-b2b/README.md`.
