Write the copy, working strictly from `04-positioning/README.md`.

- **Hero** — the one-liner, or a tightened version of it. If it needs changing to work on the page, that's a signal the positioning needed changing; say so.
- **Proof over adjectives.** Replace every "powerful", "seamless" and "intuitive" with a fact, a number, a screenshot caption or a customer sentence. If there's no proof available, use fewer words rather than better adjectives.
- **Their words, not yours.** Describe the problem the way a user would say it out loud.
- **One CTA**, repeated, not five competing ones.
- Keep sentences short. Read it back at speaking pace and cut what you stumble over.

Never write "AI-powered" as a differentiator. Everything is. Say what it does instead.
