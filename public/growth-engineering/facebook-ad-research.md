## Positioning to carry forward

Fictional Phoenix housecleaning client. Regular, deep, and move-out cleaning. Cleaner home, less housework, more space for everyday life. One action: Request a cleaning quote. Use the approved website's final colors, fonts, photography and wording.

## Two proposed video concepts

**A · Make room for your weekend — recommended.** Lead with the everyday benefit, show recognizable home and cleaning details, clarify the three services, then invite a quote. It fits the site's sunny identity and time-back positioning without making a numerical promise.

**B · Choose your home reset.** Begin with a recognizable need, then show regular, deep and move-out cleaning as distinct options. Finish on the same quote action. This is more service-led and is useful if audience feedback asks for more clarity about what the business does.

## Proposed 15-second storyboard for A

| Time | Visual | On-screen message |
| --- | --- | --- |
| 0–2s | Sunlight crossing a beautiful home; immediate confident typography | More sunshine. |
| 2–5s | Licensed cleaning detail footage or purposeful motion from approved imagery | Less housework. |
| 5–9s | Three quick, distinct service scenes; one label at a time | Regular · Deep · Move-out |
| 9–12s | A calm, clean living space; restrained camera motion | A little more room for life. |
| 12–15s | Desert Shine wordmark, clear CTA and site destination | Request a cleaning quote |

This storyboard is a proposal awaiting feedback, not an approved video. Keep text inside current placement safe areas, use readable contrast, and preview the whole film. Use properly licensed audio or keep it silent with complete on-screen meaning. Do not present stock images as actual client work or manufacture before/after evidence. Render a separate feed version when the selected placement needs it.
