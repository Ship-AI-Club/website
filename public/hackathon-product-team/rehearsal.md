# Run the sequence

## 1. Positioning & research

Help me clarify my positioning. Ask only for information missing from our shared brief, research what I do not know, and recommend a customer, offer and pricing approach for my business. Show the evidence and let me review the positioning before we continue.

## 2. Productize the site

Use our approved positioning to help me choose one useful visitor experience. Work with Product Designer on the journey, copy and design direction. Explain your recommendation and let me review the scope before building.

## 3. Design, build & review

Coordinate the engineers and designer to build our approved experience using the starter and shared brief. Show me a working preview, real desktop/phone checks and any limitations. Let me review it before deployment or activating integrations.
